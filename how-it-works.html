How it works

From a livestream moment to a published TikTok in four steps. The steps that involve TikTok are described exactly as they appear in the tool.

1. Find and edit

New GTA 6 stream moments are collected from creators whose clipping programmes allow it. Each moment is edited into a 9:16 vertical video (1080×1920, 60 fps) with captions, the creator's name and our watermark. The finished video and its caption sit on the operator's own computer, ready for review.

2. Connect TikTok (Login Kit)

  1. The operator clicks Connect TikTok in the tool.
  2. TikTok's own authorisation page opens. TikTok shows which permissions are requested: user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.
  3. After the operator approves, TikTok returns to the tool, which stores the access token on the operator's computer only.
  4. The tool calls the user info endpoint (user.info.basic) and shows the connected account's display name and avatar.

3. Review and post settings

Before each post, the tool shows a review screen that follows TikTok's Content Sharing Guidelines:

  • A preview of the video and the account (nickname and avatar) it will be posted to.
  • An editable caption with the creator credit and hashtags.
  • Who can view this video: chosen by the operator from the privacy options TikTok returns for the account. There is no preselected default.
  • Allow comments, Duet and Stitch toggles. Options the account has disabled in TikTok are shown as unavailable.
  • Commercial content disclosure (Your brand / Branded content) when a clip is part of a paid promotion, with the matching TikTok policy notice.
  • The notice "By posting, you agree to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation".

4. Publish (Content Posting API)

  • Direct Post (video.publish): the video file is uploaded to TikTok and posted to the profile with the chosen settings. The tool tells the operator that processing can take a few minutes, then checks the publish status and shows the link when the post is live.
  • Upload as draft (video.upload): the video is sent to the TikTok inbox, and the operator finishes and posts it in the TikTok app.

What the tool never does

  • It never posts without the settings above being set for that post.
  • It never accesses other TikTok users' data, followers, messages or analytics.
  • It never sells or shares data. See the Privacy Policy.
