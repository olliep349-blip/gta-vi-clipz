Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GTA VI CLIPZ Poster (also called "the app", "we" or "us"), the publishing tool of the GTA VI CLIPZ project, collects, uses, stores, shares and deletes information when it is connected to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook accounts, and when you visit this website.

1. Who we are

GTA VI CLIPZ Poster is operated by the GTA VI CLIPZ project, an independent fan project based in the United Kingdom. It is the data controller for the information described here. Contact details are in section 13.

2. Who uses the app

The app is used only by the GTA VI CLIPZ team to publish our own edited clips to accounts we own. It is not offered for public sign-up. The only platform accounts connected to it are GTA VI CLIPZ accounts.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect information about other people's accounts: no followers, following lists, messages, comments, contact lists or data of viewers. Streamers who appear in clips are public figures in their own public livestreams. We store their public channel name only so we can credit them.

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or forms. GitHub Pages, which hosts the site, may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

4. How we use information

  • To authenticate the connected account and show it in the app.
  • To upload and publish videos with the settings chosen for each post.
  • To show the post status, the link to the post and basic performance numbers for our own posts.
  • To keep the app secure and to comply with platform rules and the law.

We do not use platform data for advertising, profiling, selling, training AI models, or any purpose other than those listed above.

5. Legal basis (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

We process the information above because we have a legitimate interest in publishing and managing our own content (Article 6(1)(f)), and because it is needed to use the platform services we have signed up to (Article 6(1)(b)).

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. Information is only sent to:

  • the platform it came from (for example, a video and its settings are sent to TikTok to be posted);
  • Upload-Post (upload-post.com), a scheduling service we may use to deliver uploads to our own accounts, which processes data on our behalf;
  • authorities, where the law requires it.

7. Storage and security

Tokens and post records are stored only on the operator's own password-protected computer, in files that are not published or shared. Tokens are never shown on screen, in logs or in this website. Connections to platform APIs use HTTPS.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Access tokens are kept until they expire or the account is disconnected. They are deleted within 7 days of disconnection.
  • Post records and performance numbers for our own posts are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Platform data is refreshed at least every 30 days and is not kept longer than needed.

9. Disconnecting and your choices

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services, then remove GTA VI CLIPZ Poster.
  • Google/YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Meta: Settings, then Business integrations.

Once access is removed, the app can no longer use it, and stored data is deleted within 7 days.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to complain to a regulator (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk). To use these rights, contact us (section 13). We will reply within one month.

11. Children

The app and this website are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

12. Third-party services

The app uses TikTok for Developers, YouTube API Services, the Meta Graph API and Upload-Post. Your use of those platforms is governed by their own terms and policies, including the TikTok Privacy Policy, the YouTube Terms of Service, the Google Privacy Policy and the Meta Privacy Policy.

13. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: see Contact, or open a request at github.com/olliep349-blip/gta-vi-clipz/issues.

14. Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date. If a change is significant, we will say so at the top of this page.
