Privacy Policy: GTA VI CLIPZ Uploader

Last updated: 22 November 2026

GTA VI CLIPZ Uploader is a private tool used by one person, the owner of the GTA VI CLIPZ accounts, to post their own edited video clips to their own TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube accounts. It has no other users.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we access: with the owner's permission (Google OAuth, scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly) the tool uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and reads view and like counts for the owner's own uploads.

How it is used: only to publish the owner's own clips and to show the owner how those clips perform. No YouTube data is shared with, sold to or disclosed to any third party, and no data about other YouTube users is accessed or stored.

Storage and deletion: the OAuth token and the owner's own video statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Statistics are refreshed at least every 30 days and deleted when no longer needed. The owner can revoke access at any time from the Google security settings page; stored data is then deleted from the computer within 7 days.

TikTok, Instagram and Facebook

When the owner connects TikTok or Meta (Instagram/Facebook), the tool receives an access token that lets it upload videos to those accounts and read basic account information and post statistics. Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. The owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok settings (Security & permissions → Apps) or Meta settings (Business integrations), and delete the stored token.

General

We do not collect, sell or share personal data, we do not use cookies or tracking, and we do not access any other user's data.

Contact: via the TikTok account @gta.vi.clipz34 or the GTA VI CLIPZ YouTube channel.
