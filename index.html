GTA 6 stream highlights, edited for short-form video

GTA VI CLIPZ finds the funniest and wildest moments from Grand Theft Auto VI livestreams, edits them into vertical clips, and publishes them to the GTA VI CLIPZ accounts on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram and Facebook.

GTA VI CLIPZ Poster is the publishing tool behind those accounts. It connects to the GTA VI CLIPZ TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit and posts finished clips with the TikTok Content Posting API.

What we make

Stream highlightsShort moments from GTA 6 livestreams by creators who run official clipping programmes that allow their streams to be clipped and reposted.
Our own editEvery clip is re-edited: full gameplay frame, the streamer's webcam, word-by-word captions, trims, zooms and royalty-free music.
Always creditedEach post names and tags the streamer and links to their channel, and follows the creator's own clipping rules.

What the tool does

  • Shows the connected TikTok account (display name and avatar) so the operator always knows where a clip will be posted.
  • Lets the operator review the clip, write the caption, pick who can view it, and set the comment, Duet and Stitch options before anything is posted.
  • Uploads the video to TikTok, either straight to the profile (Direct Post) or to the TikTok inbox as a draft to finish in the TikTok app.
  • Reports back the post status and the link to the published video.

The tool only ever posts to the GTA VI CLIPZ account that authorised it. It does not read, follow, message or post to anyone else's account. See How it works and our Privacy Policy.

Content standards

  • No story spoilers, no isolated cutscenes, no cheats, glitches or exploits.
  • No hate speech: clips containing slurs are discarded automatically. Sexual content and graphic violence are screened out before posting.
  • Strong language is kept off platforms and audiences where it is not appropriate.
  • Only royalty-free or licensed music is added.
