Privacy Policy: GTA VI CLIPZ Uploader

Last updated: 20 November 2026 GTA VI CLIPZ Uploader is a private tool used by one person (the owner of the TikTok account @gta.vi.clipz34) to post their own edited video clips to their own TikTok account. What we access: when the account owner connects TikTok, the tool receives an access token that lets it upload videos to that account and read basic information about that account (display name, avatar) and its videos. How it is used: only to publish videos the owner has reviewed and approved, and to show the owner how their posts perform. Storage: tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. We do not collect, sell or share personal data, and we do not access any other TikTok user's data. Deletion: the owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok settings (Security & permissions → Apps), and delete the stored token from their computer. Contact: via the TikTok account @gta.vi.clipz34.
