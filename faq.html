Frequently asked questions

Who is GTA VI CLIPZ Poster for?

It is the in-house publishing tool of GTA VI CLIPZ, used by the GTA VI CLIPZ team to publish our own edited clips to our own accounts. It is not a public sign-up service, and it does not post on behalf of other people.

Which TikTok permissions does it use, and why?

user.info.basic shows which account is connected. video.upload sends a clip to the TikTok inbox as a draft. video.publish posts a clip directly to the connected profile.

Where do the clips come from?

From GTA 6 livestreams of creators who run clipping programmes that invite clippers to repost edited highlights. Every post credits the streamer.

I'm a streamer and want a clip removed.

Contact us (see Contact) with the link, and we will remove it from all our accounts.

How do I disconnect the app from a TikTok account?

In TikTok go to Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services, and remove GTA VI CLIPZ Poster. The stored token stops working straight away and is deleted from our computer within 7 days.

Is this affiliated with Rockstar Games?

No. It is an independent fan project.
